# Brightwave Co. - Paid Time Off & Holidays Policy

*Fictional policy created for the Copilot workshop. Version 5.0, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People & Culture.*

## 1. Paid time off (PTO)
- Full-time employees receive **25 PTO days per year**, accrued monthly.
- After **3 years of service**, employees earn **one extra PTO day for each additional year of service**, up to a
  maximum of **30 days**.
- Part-time employees receive PTO pro-rated to their contracted hours.
- You may **carry over up to 5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.
- Requests of more than 5 consecutive days should be made at least 4 weeks in advance.

## 2. Sick leave
- **10 paid sick days per year**, separate from PTO.
- A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## 3. Company holidays
- **11 company holidays** and **2 floating holidays** per year. The calendar is published each December.

## 4. Other paid leave
| Type | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Bereavement (immediate family) | 5 days |
| Bereavement (other relatives) | 3 days |
| Volunteer days | 2 days per year |
| Jury duty | Paid for the duration of service |

## 5. Workcations (working from abroad)
- Employees may combine travel and work abroad for **up to 20 working days per calendar year**.
- Give your manager at least **2 weeks' notice**. Workcation days do not use PTO.
- You must remain reachable during your local core hours.

## 6. Payout
Unused PTO is paid out when employment ends, as required by local law.

# Brightwave Co. - Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

*Fictional policy created for the Copilot workshop. Version 2.4, effective 1 March 2026. Owner: People & Culture.*

## 1. Work models
Brightwave has three work models. Your model is written in your offer letter.

| Model | What it means |
|---|---|
| Onsite | Works from a Brightwave office at least 5 days a week |
| Hybrid (default) | Works from the office **3 days a week**. Tuesday to Thursday are the recommended office days |
| Remote | Works from home. Remote employees may be invited to quarterly team meetups |

## 2. Core hours
Everyone is available for collaboration between **10:00 and 15:00** in their local time zone, regardless of work model.

## 3. Working from a different location
- Within your home country: up to **30 working days per calendar year**. Give your manager notice.
- **Working from abroad: up to 15 working days per calendar year**, with manager approval and a tax and
  legal check from People Operations. Approval takes up to 10 business days, so ask early.
- Working from abroad is not allowed in countries where Brightwave has no legal entity unless Legal approves.

## 4. Home office support
- **One-time $600** home-office setup budget for Remote and Hybrid employees (desk, chair, monitor).
- **$40 per month** connectivity allowance for Remote employees.
- Brightwave provides a laptop. Laptops are refreshed every **3 years**.

## 5. Expectations
- Be reachable during core hours and keep your calendar up to date.
- Use video for team meetings whenever possible so remote colleagues are included.
- Managers must not make promotion decisions based on office attendance alone.

## 6. Requesting changes to your work model
Talk to your manager first. Changes of work model are confirmed in writing by People & Culture.

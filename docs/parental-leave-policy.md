# Brightwave Co. - Parental Leave Policy

*Fictional policy created for the Copilot workshop. Version 3.1, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People & Culture.*

## 1. Purpose
Brightwave Co. supports employees through the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. This policy explains
paid leave, eligibility, and how to request time off.

## 2. Eligibility
- All **full-time and part-time employees** with **at least 6 months of continuous service** on the first day of leave.
- **Contractors and temporary workers are not eligible.**
- Both parents at Brightwave are eligible separately; leave is not shared between them.

## 3. Paid leave entitlement
| Role in the family | Paid leave | Pay level |
|---|---|---|
| Primary caregiver (including birth parent) | **16 weeks** | 100% of base salary |
| Secondary caregiver | **8 weeks** | 100% of base salary |

- Leave applies equally to birth, adoption, and foster placement.
- In the case of multiple births or adoptions at the same time, the entitlement does not increase.
- Employees may request up to **8 additional weeks of unpaid leave**. This requires approval from the employee's
  manager and People & Culture.

## 4. Timing and flexibility
- Leave must start within **12 months** of the birth or placement.
- Leave may be taken in **up to two blocks**.
- Employees should give **30 days' notice** where possible. Earlier leave start for medical reasons does not
  require notice.

## 5. Benefits during leave
- Health benefits continue on the same terms as during active employment.
- Paid time off (PTO) continues to accrue during paid leave.
- Equity vesting continues during paid leave and is paused during unpaid leave.

## 6. Returning to work
- Employees can request a **phased return of up to 4 weeks** at 60% of normal hours, paid at 100% of base salary.
- Employees return to the same or a comparable role.

## 7. How to request leave
1. Tell your manager and submit a request in the HR portal under *Leave of Absence*.
2. People & Culture confirms eligibility within 5 business days.
3. Your manager and People & Culture agree on a coverage plan before the leave begins.

# Brightwave Co. - Learning & Development Policy

*Fictional policy created for the Copilot workshop. Version 1.7, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People & Culture.*

## 1. Annual learning stipend
- Every employee receives a **$1,500 learning stipend per calendar year**, available after the first **90 days**
  of employment.
- Managers can approve up to **$3,000** per year when the employee provides a short business case.
- Unused stipend does **not** carry over.

## 2. What is covered
- Courses, workshops, conferences, books, and professional certification exam fees.
- Travel to a conference is covered separately under the Travel Policy.

## 3. Learning time
- Employees can spend **up to 4 hours per month** of working time on learning.
- Up to **2 days per year** can be used to attend a conference or workshop without using PTO.

## 4. Repayment clause
If you receive more than **$1,000** in a calendar year and leave Brightwave within **12 months** of the payment, you
must repay the amount above $1,000 unless you leave due to redundancy.

## 5. How to claim
1. Get your manager's approval in the HR portal before you pay.
2. Pay for the course, then submit the receipt in the HR portal under *Learning Reimbursement*.
3. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle.
